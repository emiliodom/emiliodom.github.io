Behavioral Questions

Soft skills and experience discussions.

1. Challenging Bug

Question: Tell me about the most challenging bug you've faced and how you solved it.

Tip: Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Focus on your debugging process, tools used, and what you learned.

2. Conflict Resolution

Question: Describe a time you disagreed with a team member about a technical decision.

Tip: Focus on how you communicated, how you weighed the pros and cons, and how you reached a consensus or accepted the final decision professionally.
